HYPEFORGE FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions.

Find answers to common questions about creators, brands, opportunities, campaigns, and HypeForge.

GENERAL

About HypeForge.

What is HypeForge?

HypeForge is a platform designed to connect creators with brands, sponsorships, campaigns, and collaboration opportunities.

Who is HypeForge for?

HypeForge is designed for creators, influencers, content professionals, brands, businesses, and organizations.

What can creators do?

Creators can build profiles, showcase portfolios, discover opportunities, and connect with brands.

What can brands do?

Brands can create profiles, discover creators, create campaigns, and build partnerships.

CREATORS

Creator Questions.

How do I become a creator?

Create an account using the registration page and select Creator as your account type.

Can I showcase my portfolio?

HypeForge is designed to provide creators with a professional place to showcase their work.

Can creators apply to campaigns?

Yes. The platform is designed to let creators discover relevant opportunities and apply.

Can creators work with multiple brands?

Creators can build multiple professional relationships, subject to individual campaign terms and requirements.

BRANDS

Brand Questions.

How do brands join HypeForge?

Brands can register for an account and create a professional brand profile.

Can brands create campaigns?

HypeForge is designed to support brand campaigns and creator collaborations.

How can brands find creators?

Brands can discover creators based on relevant niches, content, audiences, and campaign requirements.

ACCOUNT

Account & Access.

How do I create an account?

Go to the Register page, select your account type, enter your information, and create your account.

How do I log in?

Use the Login page and enter your account credentials.

What if I forget my password?

Password recovery can be connected to a secure backend authentication system as the platform develops.
